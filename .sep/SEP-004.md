# SEP-004: Add missing MENA and Central/South Asian countries to article tagger

**Status:** Open
**Created:** 2026-03-19
**Project:** newsroom

## Summary
article_tagger.py GEOGRAPHIC_MAPPING is missing Iran, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Syria, Yemen, Lebanon, Jordan, Libya, Tunisia, Algeria, Afghanistan, Pakistan, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Kuwait and their major cities. This means detect_countries() never returns these as country tags despite them being central to the scraper's geopolitical coverage.

## Motivation
50+ Iran-related articles collected today have no Iran country tag. Only Polymarket articles show Iran because its scraper has separate country detection. Users filtering by country in zorora miss all news articles about these countries.

## Proposed Change
Add missing countries and cities to GEOGRAPHIC_MAPPING in article_tagger.py, redeploy Lambda, trigger reindex, refresh zorora cache.

## Acceptance Criteria
Articles mentioning Iran, Iraq, Saudi Arabia etc. have correct country_tags in DynamoDB and zorora cache
